My speech worksheet:

Thank you, honorable chair and fellow delegates,

On the issue of globalization, we, the delegates of China would like to recommend that the Davos World Economic Forum on globalization take the third option, we all must work together to reduce carbon emissions.

We have come to this decision because even though our country (and its not a secret) pollutes a lot we are still getting hurt because of it. We think that every country in the world has connection to the pollution in our planet. We also know that in a couple hundred years (if we keep polluting as we do now) our planet will not be good for us to live in any more. We all need to work together to stop the pollution from consuming our planet. We need to find more ways to make electricity and energy. We need to make people who pollutes a lot pay money. We need to make international laws to save our planet from the pollution.

Thank you!
