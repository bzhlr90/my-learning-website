conditionals

If you study hard, you will pass your exams.

If she comes to my party, I will be very happy.

If=condition

Es/s=present simple

If (condition) it rains (present because of the s), I won't (future) go to the park.

If I study today, I'll go to the party tonight.

If I have enough money, I'll buy some new shoes.

She'll be late if the train is delayed.

She'll miss the bus if she doesn’t leave soon.

If I see her, I'll tell her.

Use: To talk about a future action that will probably/possibly happen.

If you put your finger in the water, the fish will bite you.

If you smoke in the hall, you will get a heavy fine.

If you adopt a pet, you will never feel lonely again.
