Sweden's 2026 eurovision song contest song

The song "My System" (representing Sweden) was written and performed Felicia (a Swedish singer). The writer was also a part of a Swedish music project called Fröken Snusk.

The song "My System" starts a bit like a regular love song and then the song gets a twist and in some point the song start saying things like "Been dancing on your grave" what shows how the song starts to transform into a breakup song.

The song has 2% of winning in the predicting of the winning (the one started even before the songs of the competition were chosen).
