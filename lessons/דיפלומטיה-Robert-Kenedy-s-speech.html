Robert Kenedy's speech

  1. 2 examples of Ethos (credibility):
    First: Kenedy sayed "We can move in that direction as a country", its showing credibility because he is saying that they are one nation.
    Second: Kenedy sayed "We will have difficult times" its showing credibility because he is saying "We are in it together".
  2. Kenedy sayed "Martin Luther King dedicated his life to love and justice", its showing he is using empathy because he is showing that Marting Luther King used empathy and later in Kenedy's speech he suggests that they will choose Martin Luther King's path (of love and justice).
  3. Kenedy sayed "there were white people responsible" its using Logos (facts) because he is saying "We are responsible but don’t blame all of us for a small group's mistake".
