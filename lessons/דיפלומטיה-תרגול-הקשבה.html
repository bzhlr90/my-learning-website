תרגול הקשבה

Every day, I get to school at 7:30 in the morning. I like to be early because it helps me start my day feeling calm and ready. I wake up around 6:30, so I have time to get my stuff together, maybe eat a quick breakfast, and head to school.

When I get there early, I have extra time to check over my homework, talk to friends, or even ask a teacher a quick question if I need help. It makes me feel ready for the day and less stressed.

Being early is helpful for me. It keeps me organized, helps me feel confident, and even helps me make a good impression on my teachers and friends. Starting my day this way makes the whole day go better but its hard to wake up this time.
