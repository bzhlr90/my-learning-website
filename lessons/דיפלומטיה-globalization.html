globalization

What I would say if I were in favour of globalization:

  • Globalization can make peace between countries.
  • Globalization makes it easier to buy something from another country.
  • Globalization can get poor people from poor places more food and support.
  • Globalization makes products cheaper

What I would say if I were against globalization:

  • Globalization makes more people work for less money.
  • Globalization makes small companies not being able to become big companies.
