Conflict – in a conflict each person or group sees things from a different perspective and the perspectives can't co-exist.

4 types of conflicts

Intra personal – a conflict between me and myself.

Inter personal – conflict between individuals.

Inter group – conflict between groups.

Intra group – conflict within the group.
