Most of the time my older brother played in it and I watched him but I wasn’t bored of it. In some ways it was even better (and funnier) to watch my older brother failing in the game over and over. After some time passed my father actually let me play on my own. In that time I realized why my brother enjoyed playing in it all the time even when I made fun of him.
