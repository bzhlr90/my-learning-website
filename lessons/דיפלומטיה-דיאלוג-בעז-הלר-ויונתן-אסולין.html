דיאלוג בעז הלר ויונתן אסולין

A shop keeper and a customer

Part 1

Customer: hey there I need to pick up my package can you bring it to me?

Shopkeeper: no

Customer: what do you mean no?

Shopkeeper: Im not going to give you your package

Customer: why?

Shopkeeper: because im too tired

Customer: can I take the package myself?

Shopkeeper: no, only staff members are allowed to enter the store room

Part 2

Customer: how will I get my package then?

Shopkeeper: come back another day. When im not around.

Customer: I cant come back another day I don’t live here I come from another part of the country!

Shopkeeper: okay okay, I understand your frustration. I will give you your package, just give me a couple of minutes.

Shopkeeper: here's your package..

Shopkeeper: whats in the thing anyway?

Customer: It is something private

Shopkeeper: I see… Interesting…

Customer: thanks for the package I guess..

Shopkeeper (quietly): finally, just get lost
