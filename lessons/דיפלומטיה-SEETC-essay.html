SEETC essay

persuasive essay:

what we need to use:

  • Emotive
  • Strong evidence
  • Connect the topic to the reader (relevance)
  • Appropriate length
  • Style (careful choice of vocabulary)
  • Preempt possible counter arguments
  • Logically structured/organized
  • Interesting

SEETC essay:

S – statement (showing what are we arguing for.)

E – explanation (explain why is it your statement.)

E – examples (an example for the explanation.)

T – tieback (repeating what I already sayed.)

C – counterargument (predicting what the other side will say to oppose your opinion. Have to be before the tieback.)
