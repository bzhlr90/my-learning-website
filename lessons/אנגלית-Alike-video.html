"Alike" video

The video shows criticism on the work and education systems. The colors in the video (not black and white) symbolize happiness and positive fillings. The video shows an adult man who is in charge of a child. The adult man sends the child to school while he (the child) is still happy and full of life. At first we see the child drawing in his school and we can see that he is happy to come back home, that what gives the adult man his color (or his happiness). Later we can see that when the child comes back from school he starts losing his color like the adult man lost his at work.
